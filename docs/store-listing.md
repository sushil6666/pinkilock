# Play Store listing and ad copy

Draft words for the Google Play listing and for a Google Ads App campaign, in English and Hindi.
Written 2026-11-27. Every claim here is something the app really does today, or is marked
**[only when live]** for parts that wait on a merge, a phone build or a sign-off.

The Hindi is a draft, not checked by a native speaker yet (the same as the app's Hindi, O13). Have
someone fluent read it once before it goes live, out loud: it should sound like a friend talking,
not a textbook.

Character limits were counted with `node -e` on the exact lines below (Play and Google Ads count
each Devanagari letter and sign as one character).

## Rules for every word in this file

Dating is a restricted category on Google Ads and is watched closely on Google Play. These keep
the listing and the ads safe to approve:

- **Never mention Spicy mode**, Saathi's Flirty tone, or anything sexual, suggestive or "tonight".
  No "hookup", "casual", "hot singles", "lonely", "near you now", no body words.
- **18+ is said plainly** in the listing. Ads are shown only to people 18 and over (set the age in
  the campaign, below).
- **Human listeners are left out of ads.** Paying a person by the minute is the part of Talk mode
  a lawyer still has to look at (O17, O18). Saathi appears only as an AI, always called an AI.
- **No "best", "#1", "top", "free"** in the title or icon, no prices in the title, no emojis in the
  title, no ALL CAPS words, no other apps' names (Tinder, Bumble, Hinge) anywhere. Play rejects these.
- **No promises the app cannot keep.** Not "verified profiles" (handle proofs are off for now), not
  "safe" as a guarantee (say what the app does instead), not "find love".
- **Prices live in Play Console's in-app products, not in the words.** Prices change; listing text
  that quotes an old price gets flagged as misleading.

## Google Ads App campaign

An App campaign takes up to 5 headlines and up to 5 descriptions, and Google mixes them. Each line
has to make sense on its own, next to any other.

### Campaign settings that keep it within the dating rules

- **Age: 18 and over only.** In the campaign's audience, exclude anyone under 18 and "unknown" age
  if Google offers that.
- **Places: the open cities only (91 today; start with the 8 metros)**, not all of India. People elsewhere would sign up to an empty
  Discover.
- **Languages: English and Hindi** to start, with the matching words below.
- Before the first campaign, read Google Ads' policy page for dating and companionship ads in the
  Policy center and check whether the account must apply for permission first. Google changes this
  policy from time to time, so check it on the day rather than trusting this file.
- Images and videos: the same rules as the words. Fully dressed people, faces from stock or mock
  data only, no couples in bed, no close-ups of bodies.

## Steps for Sushil, when the Play listing goes in

1. Play Console, the app, **Grow users > Store presence > Main store listing**. Paste the English
   name, short description and full description (without the `[only when live]` markers, and
   without those lines if the part is not live).
2. **Add translation > Hindi (hi-IN)** on the same page, and paste the Hindi versions.
3. Upload the screenshots and the feature graphic for each language.
4. **Policy and programs > App content**: target audience 18 and over only; fill in the content
   rating questions honestly (it has user-to-user contact and in-app purchases); fill in Data
   safety from `docs/data-map.md`.
5. Pink Gold and Boost prices go in **Monetize > Products**, not in the description. Phone apps
   must sell them through Play Billing (O15) before they can show in the app.
6. Ads come after the listing is approved: in Google Ads, **New campaign > App promotion > App
   installs**, pick Pinkilock, then use the settings and lines above.
